Charts and downloads use simplified tracks (~11 m tolerance) — fine for navigation; full-resolution GPX files are in the chat. The weather badge on each stage shows a 10-year average for that date until the day comes inside the forecast window, then switches to the live forecast on every load — the arrow is wind relative to your course, so pointing up is a tailwind. “Show where I am” puts your position on the matching stage map and elevation curve and compares it with that day’s checkpoints; it needs location permission, works only on the day itself, and the coordinates stay in your browser — there is no server to send them to. Open this once on wifi and it is cached for offline use: the plan, the maps you have scrolled past, and the GPX all work with no signal, and the weather falls back to the averages. “Alt route” hands the stage’s start and finish to cycle.travel if you need to rebuild the day around a closure.

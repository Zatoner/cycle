## Day-by-day (canonical itinerary)
- **Stage 1 · Sat 29 Aug — UK leg to Portsmouth.** Options, decided at breakfast:
  (a) Guildford start 80.6 km / +734 m (original GPX);
  (b) **Preferred new draft:** Petersfield → Portsmouth ~58 km via East Meon / Meon valley,
      Titchfield, Lee-on-the-Solent, **Stokes Bay (swim)**, **Gosport–Portsmouth harbour ferry**
      (every 15 min, ~£2 + bike, 4 min crossing) → The Hard → port.
      ⚠ cycle.travel "Any" profile includes ~10 km unpaved (Meon Valley Trail near Soberton) —
      fine on 28mm+ tyres dry, otherwise re-plot with "Paved" toggle. One real climb
      (Old Winchester Hill shoulder, ~180 m, short pushing section flagged).
  (c) Bail-out: train straight to Portsmouth & Southsea, 3 km roll.
  Ferry check-in 19:15, sail 20:15. Night aboard, 4-berth cabin.
- **Stage 2 · Sun 30 — St Malo → Mohon. 102.3 km, +535 m.** Dock 08:45, roll 09:30, Dinan coffee
  km 26 (Rue du Jerzual), arrive ~17:30 (worst 18:30). Grandparents. Josselin castle 15 km if evening allows.
- **Stage 3 · Mon 31 — Mohon → Quiberon via Carnac. 109.4 km, +708 m.** Roll 09:00-09:30.
  Auray/Saint-Goustan crêpe km 71 (~13:30), Carnac Ménec alignments km 86 (~15:30, route passes 100 m away),
  Côte Sauvage optional down the peninsula. Quiberon ~17:30-18:30. **Boat 20:45** (big buffer). 
- **Tue 1 – Thu 3 — Belle-Île.** Pointe des Poulains, Côte Sauvage road, Port Donnant (swim, sea ~18°C),
  Le Palais aperitif. Island laps 45-85 km optional. Thursday evening swim recommended (early Friday start).
- **Stage 4 · Fri 4 — Quiberon → Rosporden. 104.8 km, +758 m.** Boat 09:45→10:35, roll ~10:50.
  Saint-Cado detour km 30 (2 km off-route — may be sacrificed given later boat), Lorient km 51.
  Rosporden ~18:00-18:30. Camp.
- **Stage 5 · Sat 5 — Rosporden → Roscoff. 109.6 km, +1,234 m. Queen stage.** Roll 09:00.
  Monts d'Arrée crossing, summit 362 m at km 68 (highest point of trip; cold descent — gilet).
  St-Pol-de-Léon (Kreisker spire) km 104. Roscoff old port ~17:30. Night in Roscoff.
- **Sun 6 — Home.** Check-in 08:45, sail 09:45 (Pont-Aven), Plymouth 14:00. Millbay → station = 2 km,
  10 min, ~25 min buffer on the 15:15. Paddington 18:24, home ~19:00.

## Website — current state & conventions
- Single self-contained `index.html`. Leaflet 1.9.4 (cdnjs) + OSM tiles; SVG chart fallback on tile error.
  CSS svg rules are scoped (`svg.prof`, `.mapbox>svg`) — do NOT add a global `svg{}` rule (breaks Leaflet overlay).
- Features: at-a-glance table; stage cards (map, elevation profile, timeline w/ sunsets, effort ratings,
  weather badge = 10-yr average until date enters forecast window then live; wind arrow relative to course);
  "Show where I am" geolocation vs checkpoints (client-side only); offline caching (service worker);
  editable budget with live total (in-memory only); booking checklist; **one button per stage: Download GPX**
  (earlier Share/cycle.travel buttons intentionally removed — do not re-add; an "Alt route · cycle.travel"
  handoff link exists per stage per latest deployed version).
- Style: no emojis on site, marine navy/fog palette, mono for times/stats, concise mobile-first.
- Deploy: commit to main, Pages builds ~1 min; verify live page changed (CDN caches ~10 min).
